# Bots (`bots`, slice 6): v2 bot configs, the general engine (scout, manager, guard), candidates, Run now, the version editor, the Trade recommendation card and the FU-1 spend caps

> Before this slice, each user had one fixed, rules-only bot, Slack Water, with a single v1 version and Pause/Resume. Nothing created or edited a bot, no bot had acted on a signal, and Trade's recommendation card read "Arrives with bots". Slice 6 made bots versioned configs that the owner creates and tunes.
> - **Create and change.** A bot is created from one of four templates, or cloned. Every change saves a new version (`v{seq}·{hash[0:4]}`) in a new **v2 vocabulary** (`"schema": "bot-config.v2"`).
> - **Two engines.** The bot runner runs a general engine for v2 configs beside the unchanged 2b pass, which still runs v1 configs.
> - **Three loops per general bot.**
>   - The **scout** wakes on a schedule or on signals. It ranks and caps per asset class before any model call, then writes **candidates**, each with a thesis and no order.
>   - The **manager** watches candidates on closed bars. It enters, adds, trims and exits through the bot-side intake.
>   - The **guard** checks stops, invalidation, daily loss and exposure on every 30 s pass.
> - **Probation.** New bots and new versions start on a 30-day probation (`observing`): decisions are recorded and no order is placed.
> - **For the owner.** *Run now* runs one loop on demand. The Trade card shows the followed bot's candidate with *Use this order* and *Not this time*.
>
> **Migration J** (`20261020041046_bots_candidates_and_requests`) adds `candidates`, `bot_version_events`, `bot_run_requests` and `bots.probation_until`. It re-creates six CHECKs and seeds two model routes and one price row.
>
> **Service.** Fourteen new gated, owner-scoped routes (59 gated routes in all), a v2 validator that refuses by dotted field, the templates as pinned data, the `recommendations.follow` setting, `candidate_id` on orders and skips, seven new error codes (five from contracts r1, two from FU-1), and `RequiredSchemaVersion` → J.

> **Bot.** `packages/waves`, a TypeScript port of the service's Elliott/regime labeller that is byte-identical on the five fixtures. Also: dispatch by vocabulary, the scheduler, a per-bot lock, run requests, scout, manager, guard, the candidate lifecycle, the probation end, realtime requests, the `bot_decide`/`bot_thesis` model stages and the C4'' close stamp.

### Problem

The product's core idea is bots the owner creates and tunes against evidence. In practice, that means:
- picking the sources and tuning their weights beside each source's results;
- picking a decision tier;
- setting a policy, a universe, an allocation and a schedule;
- saving that as a version, and watching it scout, enter and manage positions on paper.

At `main` on 2026-10-16 (service `1547a89`, web `918d7b6`, bot `ece175a`, migrations at I), the runner evaluated only v1 zone bots every 30 s. It had no scheduler, no per-bot lock and no candidates. `decisions` already carried `loop`, `run_id`, `candidate_id`, `signal_ids`, `signal_snapshot`, `model`, `market_state` (with empty `elliott` and `regime` slots) and `exit_reason`, but no `candidates` table existed. The Elliott/regime labeller lived only in the service, and the bot cannot call the service.

The roadmap's slice-6 exit test:
- two auto bots run 7 days on paper without intervention;
- scout wakes produce candidates and never orders;
- a guard stop is honoured within 2 minutes, whatever the manager's cadence;
- any config change creates a new version;
- attribution numbers reconcile with the decision record.

### Spec decisions that shaped the build (PRD S1–S24, selected)

- **S1, dispatch by vocabulary.** A config whose canonical form equals the C10 v1 canonical runs the 2b pass. One with `"schema": "bot-config.v2"` runs the general engine. Anything else is `version_unsupported`.
- **S2, classes.** `stock` on the `set` calendar is `th_stock`; any other `stock` is `us_stock`; `crypto` is `crypto`; `gold` is `gold`. `fx` is never in a universe.
- **S3, tier `none`.** A fourth decision tier, "Rules only", makes no model call.
- **S4, caps before calls.** The scout ranks and caps per class before any model call. Each survivor then gets exactly one `decide()` and one thesis call.
- **S6, gates are verdicts.** An unmet entry condition is a watch verdict, not a decision. Only a hard limit that blocks an otherwise-met entry writes a `skip`.
- **S7, direct entries.** Rules-only configs enter directly on a zone turn.
- **S8 and S11, the only bot writes to `bots.status`.** The guard pauses a bot; the runner ends probation.
- **S9, C4''.** The close stamp's `exit_reason` comes from the closing decision's `policy_result.exit_reason`.
- **S10, Run now through a table.** *Run now* writes `bot_run_requests`; there is no HTTP between the service and the bot.
- **S12, realtime.** The bot writes `instruments.realtime_requested_at`.
- **S15, the sparkline.** It comes from the 1m portfolio the summary already computes.
- **S17, template thesis.** A failed thesis call never blocks a candidate; the thesis becomes a template sentence.
- **S18, no manager model call.** The manager makes no model call in this slice.
- **S19, a per-bot advisory lock** around each bot's evaluation.
- **S20, attribution's SQL.**
- **S21, `me`'s sized quantity.**
- **S23, the scout's signal window.** It is the signals since the previous scout run's `started_at`; signal-driven wakes run at most once per 5 minutes.
- **S24, closed `counts` keys** per loop.

### The FU-1 spend caps (ORCH-10, contracts r2)

The service and bot security reviews raised the same risk: one bot could exhaust the shared daily model-spend ceiling (`MODEL_SPEND_DAILY_CEILING_USD`, default USD 1.00, which the signals pipeline also uses), and a signal-driven scout can wake every 5 minutes. On 2026-10-21 at 00:40Z the owner asked for FU-1 in this build. Each plan gained new steps after its completed ones (service S11–S12, bot S15–S16, web S16–S17). The completed steps' contract entries stayed byte-identical, so their evidence carried over, and the work rode the same branches and PRs. Contracts r2 is a changelog over r1. C12, C11', C13, C4'', J's schema and the route list are unchanged.

1. **Per-class caps.**
   - `limits.max_candidates.<class>` is a whole number in 0–10, and `limits.max_symbols.<class>` in 0–20.
   - The service refuses a value over its cap as `400 invalid_request` `{field: "config.limits.max_candidates.<class>" | "config.limits.max_symbols.<class>", reason: "out_of_range"}`. This is a value rule (after the grammar's `invalid`), checked `max_symbols` before `max_candidates`, classes in order.
   - The bot's v2 reader refuses a stored config outside the bounds as `version_unsupported`.
   - The web editor checks the same bounds and shows "Use 10 or fewer." or "Use 20 or fewer.".
   - The four templates (5 candidates and 4 symbols per class) and the Upgrade translation (6 and 6) stay valid.
2. **At most 10 active auto bots per account.**
   - Active means `kind = 'auto'` and `status IN ('running','observing')`. The manual `me` bot and paused bots do not count.
   - `POST /bots`, `POST …/clone` and `POST …/resume` refuse an 11th with `409 bot_limit_reached` `{limit: 10}` and write nothing.
   - The count is taken under `pg_advisory_xact_lock(MeBotLockKey(user))`. Resume now takes that lock before its `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` row lock.
   - Web copy: "You have 10 active bots. Pause one to start another."
3. ***Run now*: 6 per bot and loop in a rolling 60 minutes.**
   - The window counts `bot_run_requests.requested_at`, taken or not, and is checked after `run_pending`.
   - The 7th request is `429 run_rate_limited` `{retry_after_s}`: the whole seconds until the oldest of the six leaves the window, rounded up and at least 1. The same value goes out as an integer `Retry-After` header.
   - Nothing is written and no audit event fires.
   - Web toast: "Run now is limited to 6 runs an hour. Try again in {n} min.", where {n} = `ceil(retry_after_s / 60)`.
4. **Per-bot daily model budget (bot only, no schema or environment change).**
   - `BOT_DAILY_CEILING_SHARE = 0.25` × the ceiling, per Bangkok day, on the two bot stages.
   - The budget day is the Bangkok day of the wake's `started_at`. This was the plan-review r7 ruling against an unbounded midnight carry-over.
   - Spend = `scoutCostForDay` (the sum of the day's other scout runs' `counts.cost_usd`) + a carried half-done survivor's held reservations + the wake's own tally.
   - Before each survivor's calls, its estimate is added. Over the budget, that survivor and every later one take the template with no call and nothing reserved.
   - The budget is visible as `bots_bot_budget_reached_total{tier}` (exported at 0 for `jev`, `small_llm` and `large_llm`) and as the warn log `bots_bot_budget_reached` (`bot_id` and `run_id` only, once per wake).
   - Tier `none` is exempt, and the global ceiling still applies on top.

### The bot engine (`tidewatch-bot`, `packages/bots/src/general/`)

**Pass order** (`runEnginePass`, every `BOTS_EVAL_INTERVAL_MS` = 30 s under `runner:bots`; passes never overlap):
1. **Take the run requests.** Pending requests are taken in one statement (`takeRunRequests`).
2. **End due probations.** `endProbations` runs its own statement: `status = 'running'`, `status_since = now()` and `probation_until = NULL` for `observing` bots whose `probation_until <= now()`. It writes `probation_ended` and `bot_status_changed {observing → running}` with a null actor.
3. **Read the bots to evaluate**: `kind = auto`, `status IN (running, observing)`.
4. **Evaluate each bot under its own lock.** The lock is `bot:<bot_id>`, taken with `tryLock` on the `LockSession` that holds `runner:bots`, outside any transaction, and released in a `finally`. It uses no pooled connection, so `SERVE_POOL_MAX` stays 12. A bot whose lock is held elsewhere logs `bots_lock_busy` and is skipped, and its taken requests wait in memory.
5. **Dispatch by vocabulary.** `dispatchVersion` reads `current_version` once per bot per pass:
   - `legacy` (exactly the v1 config) runs the unchanged 2b evaluation;
   - `general` (accepted by `readConfigV2` and hashing to `current_version`) runs guard, then manager, then scout (when due or requested);
   - anything else is `unsupported`: one `failed` run per (bot, version) per runner run, and a run request gets one `failed` manual run.

Without the worker's general dependencies, the 2b `runPass` runs alone.

**Start-up.** Before the first pass, the runner does two things:
- Runs left `running` for more than 5 minutes are finished `failed`, `error = interrupted`, with **their own loop's** zero counts. This was GAP-B3, fixed in review.
- Every request taken before this start without a run is released (`releaseRunRequests(now)`).

**Scheduler (PD7, S23).**
- **`times`:** `next_wake` is the next configured `HH:MM` in Bangkok strictly after the newest scout run's `started_at` (else `status_since`). Missed wakes collapse into one.
- **`signal_driven`:** a wake is due when a qualifying signal arrived after the last scout start and that start was at least 5 minutes ago.
- **`off`:** never.
- A paused bot is not evaluated, so it never wakes.

**Scout** (PRD §4.4; `scout.ts`, `models.ts`):
1. **Universe.** The resolved universe for the class, minus instruments the bot holds or has a live candidate on. It is resolved once per evaluation.
2. **Signal-driven candidates.** `up` signals in the window with a weighted impact ≥ `scout.min_impact` and a horizon in `policy.horizons`, grouped per instrument (at most 20 signal ids). A `down` signal over the threshold writes a `propose` decision with `direction = short`, reason `long_only` and no candidate (D12).
3. **Setup-driven candidates.** Universe instruments whose strip zone run began after the previous wake and reads `buy` or `pre_buy_2`.
4. **Rank and cap before any model call (S4).**
   - The score is `impact_w × allocation[class]`.
   - Instruments with no venue rule or price feed, or whose class has reached `max_symbols`, are dropped. `max_symbols` counts held and live-candidate instruments once each.
   - Instruments with a null `ttl_until` are dropped before the cap.
   - Then `max_candidates[class]` survivors are kept per class.
5. **Decide**, unless the tier is `none`: one `bot_decide` call per survivor. The candidate's `p` is the tier's answer.
6. **Thesis**, unless the tier is `none`: one `bot_thesis` call (strict JSON, at most 240 characters, prompt `bot_thesis.v1`). On any failure, a ceiling refusal or a budget refusal, the thesis is a template sentence with `thesis_source = template` (S17).
7. **Market state and levels.**
   - `market_state` carries `elliott` and `regime` from `packages/waves`, on the manager timeframe.
   - `invalidation_px` is the first ranked bullish candidate's invalidation, else `close − stop_atr × atr14`.
   - `ttl_until` is `ttl_sessions[class]` sessions ahead on the instrument's calendar.
8. **Write, one transaction per survivor:** the `propose` decision, the candidate (`proposed`), and `instruments.realtime_requested_at` for an instrument off the watchlist.

The scout never reads money and never places an order.

**Bounded model calls and carried wakes** (fix rounds 2 and 3):
- Each call has a deadline (`MODEL_CALL_TIMEOUT_MS`, 30 s in the bound's arithmetic). A call cut by it is charged at its estimate (`CallDeadlineError` → `lost_after_send`).
- The pass's `SCOUT_MODEL_BUDGET_MS` (15 s) never cuts a call. Once it is spent, no new call starts, and a model-tier wake is **carried**: the run stays `running`, and the next evaluation continues the wake before any new one. A survivor can be carried half-done, at its thesis, with its paid decide held.
- The guard's worst-case delay is 2 × (15 + 30) s + W = **90 s + W**, where W is the non-model pass work. A spec proves the bound with W = 0, using every call hanging 30 s, 3 bots and 16 passes.
- A carried wake that ends (pause, new version, the runner stopping) finishes `failed`/`interrupted`.
- A survivor that throws after a call still records its calls (`recordBotCalls`) and counts them in the run and the per-bot budget (FU-1 fix F1). Its reservations are always released.

**Manager** (PRD §4.5; `manager.ts`, `positions.ts`, `direct-entry.ts`, `intake.ts`):
- **Triggers.** It acts on an instrument only on a new closed bar of `schedule.manager.timeframe` (`strip` means the strip timeframe), or, with `on_events`, on an event: a new strong signal, a strip zone change, or `px:last` crossing a level. It reads the universe's newest bars once per pass (`latestIndicatorRows`).
- **Candidates.**
  - `proposed → watching` (reason `seen`) on the first evaluation.
  - Expiry at TTL: `expired`, reason `ttl`.
  - A close at or below `invalidation_px` before entry: `expired`, reason `invalidated`.
  - The entry condition is checked on the closed bar.
  - The gates run in order: `cdc_filter`, `elliott_filter`, `impact_below_min`, `p_below_min`, `max_open`, `max_symbols`, `allocation`. A failed gate is a `gated` verdict, not a decision.
  - Sizing follows PD4, capped by the fee-net allocation room (ORCH-9c).
  - The entry goes through the intake with `client_order_id = UUIDv5("{bot_id}|{instrument_id}|candidate|{candidate_id}")`, copying the proposing decision's signals, snapshot and model (ORCH-9a). On `placed`, the candidate becomes `entered`, with `stop_px = entry − stop_atr × atr14`.
  - An observing bot writes a skip-shaped decision with `reasons ["observing"]` once per candidate (ORCH-9b), and the candidate stays `watching`.
- **Positions.**
  - Exit on zone, horizon (`hold_max_sessions`) or invalidation.
  - *Add*, at most once per signal.
  - *Trim* of half on a `down` signal.
  - Each close moves the candidate to `closed` with its reason and clears `realtime_requested_at`.
  - An `entered` candidate with no position and no pending order (a cancelled entry, a user close) is closed as `closed (user)`.
- **Direct entries (S7).** With `scout.kind = off` and `entry_condition = zone_turns_buy`, a strip Buy run creates the candidate `proposed → entered` and the order in one intake transaction. This keeps the 2b idempotency key and skip reasons.
- **Version change.** The service never touches candidates on a save. The bot expires the previous version's live candidates with reason `version` on its next pass.

**Guard** (PRD §4.6; `guard.ts`). On every pass, for each general bot that is `running` or `observing`, with rules only, from the recorded `px:last` (fresh within 120 s; a stale price is `deferred` with `["price_unavailable"]`):
- **Stop** (`last ≤ stop_px`) or **invalidation** (`last ≤ invalidation_px`): a protective market close with `loop guard`, `action close`, and `policy_result {rule: "protect", exit_reason, level, last}`.
- **Daily loss** (`equity ≤ day-start equity × (1 − daily_loss_pct)`): close every position. The pause waits until every open position has a close placed. Then `bots.status = paused`, with event `guard_paused {day_pnl_pct}`.
- **Total exposure and class ceiling** (`× 1.10`): close the newest positions, `exit_reason protect`, counted as `trimmed`.

The guard writes a run only when it acted, or on a manual run. Its `protected` entries are written even when a pause skips the manager (GAP-B2, fixed in review). A guard pause skips the manager and the scout for that pass. The pause (`pauseFromGuard`) and the probation flip are short transactions of their own, never under an account lock. Legacy bots have no guard.

**Candidate lifecycle (PRD §4.7).** `proposed` (scout) → `watching` (manager) → `entered` (order) → `closed` (`zone | horizon | invalidation | stop | protect | user`). A `proposed` or `watching` candidate can also become `expired` (`ttl | invalidated | version`). For `me`, *Not this time* sets `declined_at`/`declined_decision_id`, and *Use this order* sets `followed_order_id`. Neither changes `state`. ## API

### Conventions

- **Gating.** Every new route mounts on the gated market group: `NoStore → ReadyGate → BodyLimit → RequireSession → RequireRole("admin") → RequirePasswordChanged`. CSRF is engine-wide on POST and PUT.
- **Body limits.** `POST /bots` and `POST /bots/{bot_id}/versions` use a separate config group with a 64 KiB body limit; every other route keeps 8 KiB.
- **Scope.** Every repository query is scoped by `owner_user_id`. A foreign or absent bot is one indistinguishable `404 not_found` (`TestBots_NewRoutes_ScopeAndNotFound`, 11 `:bot_id` routes).
- **Shapes.** Queries and bodies are strict (an unknown key is `400 unknown_field`, never named). Fields are snake_case, decimals are strings, instants are RFC 3339 UTC, and absent values are `null`.
- **Gated count.** `TestRouter_ForcedChangeGatesNewRoutes` counts **59** gated routes: 45 + 14, of which 56 are production and 3 are test probes. The build prompt's "+13" was a count slip (ORCH-5d).
- **The manual bot.**
  - Clone, versions POST, run and fit-weights answer `400 invalid_request {reason: manual_bot}`.
  - `GET …/versions` answers `{"items": []}`, and the candidates read answers an empty page.
  - The version detail, upgrade, attribution, schedule and universe reads answer `404`.
- **A legacy (v1) bot.**
  - Candidates: an empty page. Upgrade: answers.
  - Attribution weights read `"0"`.
  - Schedule: `scout.kind "off"`, `manager.timeframe "strip"`.
  - Universe: the translation's universe.

### Behaviour worth knowing

- **Create and clone.**
  - One transaction under the user's `MeBotLockKey`, in this order: the active count (`bot_limit_reached`), the name (1–40 runes after trimming, no control character, unique case-insensitively), then the writes.
  - The writes: the bot, its paper account, a benchmark snapshot, the initial mark, v1 from the template's embedded JSON (`news_drift` v1 is `v1·ef8d`), the `created`/`cloned` and `probation_skipped` events, audit `bot_created`/`bot_cloned`, and the log `paper_account_created`.
  - A clone copies the source's **current** config as v1, with its template, label, starting capital and the limit columns. The review fold closed a race in which the clone read the bot row and then the version unlocked (`TestService_CloneBot_SaveInterleaved`).
- **Save as vN.**
  - The validator refuses in r1 §2.3 order. The first failure wins, reported by dotted field and a reason from the closed set `missing`, `unknown_field`, `invalid`, `out_of_range`, `duplicate`, `unknown`, `timeframe_unavailable` (`4h` only).
  - A set-like array out of canonical order (`source_weights`, `include`, `exclude`, `horizons`, `schedule.scout.times`) is refused, never reordered, because array order is part of the C10 hash.
  - The canonical form and hash go through the existing C10 code.
  - One transaction locks the bot row `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, inserts `seq = max + 1`, moves `current_version` and writes `max_position_pct`/`max_total_exposure` from `limits`. It applies the status rules, writes the events and audit `bot_version_created`, and (after the review fold) counts and logs a status change.
  - `ValidateConfigV2`'s table has 175 cases.
- **Schedule.** The summaries are service text that the web prints as sent: "{when}: {n} signal(s) → {n} candidate(s)", "every {tf} · last {HH:mm} · {action}", "no breach · checked {HH:mm}". A request is `stale` when `taken_at IS NULL` and it is at least 10 minutes old. `next_wake` is computed in Bangkok (fixed UTC+07:00).
- **Candidates cursor.** Its own kind byte `C` and a state byte go into the checksum, bound to the bot (`TestCandidatesCursor_KindAndState`).
- **Universe.** The PRD §4.11 resolution for the current version:
  - lists and watchlist, plus `include`, minus `exclude`;
  - filtered to active instruments with a venue rule, not `fx`, not manual-fill and not futures;
  - the liquidity floor over the newest 20 daily bars;
  - plus held instruments.
- **Attribution (S20).** `n`, `n_graded` and `avg_r_5d` (6 decimal places) come from one statement over every version's `actor = 'bot'`, `action IN ('buy','add')` decisions per `signal_snapshot.source_code`. `graded` means `n_graded ≥ 20`. `TestBots_Attribution_MatchesSQL` compares the route with the SQL.
- **Fit weights.** A logistic fit by Newton's method with L2 λ = 1, at most 50 iterations, fitted values clamped to `[0.05, 1.00]`. It writes nothing (`TestBots_FitWeights_WritesNothing`).
- **Model tiers.**
  - `measured`: the mean `cost_usd` over the last 7 Bangkok days when `calls_7d ≥ 20`.
  - `list`: 1,500 input tokens for Jev and small; 2,500 input and 300 output for large.
  - Then × `usd_thb`, to 4 decimal places.
  - No price row or no rate → `unavailable: true`.
- **Recommendation and follow (S14, ORCH-5c).**
  - `PUT /me/settings/recommendations.follow` checks the shape only: `{"mode": "champion"}` or `{"mode": "bot", "bot_id": "<uuid>"}`. Ownership is resolved at read time.
  - **Resolution:** `bot` → that bot when it is the user's auto bot. Otherwise the champion rule: the user's `champion = true` auto bot, else the oldest `running` or `observing` auto bot whose current version is v2 with a scout, else none.
  - **The candidate shown:** the followed bot's newest `watching` candidate, then the newest `proposed`, long, not declined.
  - **`sized` (S21):** the followed bot's policy applied to `me`'s equity, floored to the lot. It is `null` when there is no usable price.
  - **`reasons`:** four strings (the thesis, the strongest signal, the CDC zone and age, the Elliott or regime line).
  - **`declined`:** true when no candidate is shown and the followed bot has a live candidate the user declined.
  - **`invalidated`:** set for 10 minutes after an invalidation expiry.
- **Candidate citation.** `check()` reads a cited candidate right after the instrument check and before everything else, for placement, estimate and skip alike. The refusal is `422 candidate_not_applicable {reason: unknown | instrument_mismatch | closed}`, and nothing is written. `signal_ids` must be `[]` or the candidate's ids as a set.
  - **On an order:** the decision records `candidate_id`, `followed_recommendation`, and the proposing decision's `signal_snapshot`/`model`. `followed_order_id` is set as the transaction's last write, when still null.
  - **On a skip:** `loop user`, the candidate's `signal_ids`, `followed_recommendation false`. `declined_at` is set once, plus audit `recommendation_declined`.
  - **Not compared:** the skip's direction against the candidate's (FU-2).

### `BotSummary` additions (contracts §8.1)

Six keys follow `last_run`:
- **`probation {until, day, of: 30}`**, or `null`.
- **`champion`.**
- **`candidates_open`:** the live count, any version.
- **`next_wake`:** `null` for `signal_driven`, `off`, legacy, manual and paused bots.
- **`strategy {vocabulary, sources_weighted, decision_tier}`**, or `null` for the manual bot.
- **`sparkline`:** at most 30 `day_end` marks `{day, equity}`, oldest first; `[]` for a fresh bot.
